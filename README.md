## Research

My research focuses on relational reconstruction, local-to-global obstructions, and structural invariants across discrete, homological, and arithmetic settings, combining proof-complete mathematical development with computer-assisted formalization in **Lean**.

Current directions:

- Relational reconstruction and defect/no-go theorems on marked topological pairs and finite relational systems
- Hilbert chain complexes, integral flow lattices, and discrete homological obstructions
- Arithmetic and automorphic realizations, including harmonic Maass forms
- Machine-checked formal verification of foundational definitions and cross-layer bridge theorems in Lean

## Monograph & Formal Verification

**Local Information in Global Structures: Relational Reconstruction, Hilbert Defects, and Arithmetic Realization** *(2026)*  
Unpublished research monograph (~509 pp., currently under editorial assessment; restricted archival DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.24356791`). Develops a self-contained reconstruction framework from marked topological pairs to Hilbert chain complexes, integral flow lattices, and arithmetic/automorphic realizations, together with explicit defect and no-go theorems.

**Companion Lean Formalization** *(2026)*  
Machine-checked proofs covering foundational structures and selected cross-layer bridge theorems from the monograph. Hosted in the pinned repository below.
